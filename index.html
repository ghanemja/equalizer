    The math, explained

    Every sound is an equation

    A sound plays its equation every time one of its squares is lit. The equation's output is the sound wave: a number between −1 and 1, computed 44,100 times a second. The knobs are the numbers in the equation.

    Try sin(TAU * f * tau) * exp(-tau * 8): a sine wave at the note's pitch that fades out.

    Variables

    Functions

    Oscillators take a phase in cycles: saw(f*tau), sqr(f*tau, 0.25), tri(f*tau). noise() is white noise. ad(tau, attack, decay) is an envelope. a ^ b is a power, and c ? a : b picks a or b.

    Patterns as text

    Drums: x...x... (x hits, . waits). Notes: C3 . E3 . G3+B3 - (notes or chords joined by +, - stops the note). Each sound's pattern text is under Rhythm.
