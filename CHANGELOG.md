# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Equation language with a whitelisted parser: variables, functions, oscillators, envelopes, noise, parameters.
- Step patterns for drums and notes/chords, exact-time triggers (`at`) and gate equations.
- Offline rendering (`renderSong`, `toWav`) and live playback with hot-swap (`Player`).
- Track effects: gain, pan, low-pass, high-pass, echo.
- 25 built-in instrument equations and three example songs.
- DJ-studio web app: knobs, step pads, oscilloscope, meters, WAV export, share links.
